Privacy Policy

Trekta – Map Your World  ·  Last updated: November 2026

Summary: Trekta has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no advertising. The developer does not collect any of your data. Your trips, places, routes, photos and GPS positions are stored only on your device. To show the map, the app downloads map pictures from OpenFreeMap, and place-name searches in Explore go to OpenStreetMap (see section 7). Like any website, those services can see your device's internet address and which area or place is being looked up.

1. Who We Are

Trekta is a personal mapping and journey-logging app developed by an independent developer. It is available as a Progressive Web App (PWA) and may be offered through the Google Play Store.

2. What Data the App Stores on Your Device

Trekta stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your profile: name, mobile number and email address, if you choose to enter them in Settings (they are used only in reports and exports you create)
  • Trips (maps) you create, including their names, descriptions and types
  • Places you log, including GPS coordinates, altitude, type, status, rating, notes and photos
  • Routes you record, as GPS points with times and altitudes
  • Photos taken or selected within the app
  • App settings and preferences, such as Sunlight mode and quick buttons
  • Map pictures you have viewed, kept so the map works without signal

This data is stored in your browser's localStorage, IndexedDB and Cache Storage, which are standard on-device storage. It never leaves your device unless you choose to export or share it.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

The developer does not collect, receive or have access to:

  • Your name, email address or any contact details
  • Your GPS location, your places or your routes
  • Your photos or any media
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Device identifiers

4. GPS and Location Data

Trekta uses your device's GPS to show your position on the map, record routes and log the position of places. Your GPS coordinates are processed on your device and are never sent to the developer or to any other service.

However, to draw the map the app downloads the map pictures for the area on screen. On the Map tab this is usually the area around you, so the map service could tell roughly which area you are viewing. Map pictures you have already viewed are reused from your device without being downloaded again.

You can use the app without granting location permission, but GPS features (live position, route recording, logging coordinates) will not be available.

5. Camera, Photos and Clipboard

Trekta may ask for access to your camera and photo library so that you can attach photos to places and trips and scan QR codes. Photos are stored on your device in IndexedDB and are never uploaded.

When you tap a copy button, the position shown is placed on your device's clipboard so that you can paste it into another app. Trekta does not read your clipboard.

6. Exported and Shared Data

Important: When you use the Export feature to create a ZIP, GPX, report or photo gallery, or share a place by QR code, the exported data may contain your name, contact details, GPS coordinates and photos. If you choose to share it (for example by WhatsApp or email), you are responsible for that sharing. Only share with people you trust — GPS coordinates show exactly where you have been.

7. Third-Party Services

All of the app's software libraries are built into the app, so nothing is downloaded from code libraries when it starts. The app only contacts the services below, and only when you use the feature they provide:

ServiceUsed forWhat it receives
OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org)The map on the Map tab and in ExploreRequests for map pictures of the area shown, and your internet address
OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org)Place-name search in ExploreThe words you type into the search box, and your internet address. Grid reference and coordinate searches are worked out on your device and are not sent.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors; map styles © OpenMapTiles. None of your trips, places, photos, routes or GPS positions are sent to these services. Each service is responsible for its own handling of the requests it receives under its own privacy policy.

The app does not use Google Analytics, advertising networks or any tracking services.

8. Children's Privacy

Trekta is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

9. Data Security

Since your data is stored on your device, its security depends on the security of your device. We recommend using a screen lock and keeping your device's operating system up to date. Make regular backups using Export, because data stored on a device can be lost.

If you use the Reset All Local Data function in Settings, all app data on that device is permanently deleted.

10. Your Rights

Since the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing for us to provide, correct or delete on your behalf. All your data is on your device and under your full control. You can delete it at any time using the Reset function in Settings, or by clearing the app's data on your device.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be shown on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
